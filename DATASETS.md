# Official data sources

The HeritageGuard paper uses two separately published **raw Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG) monitoring datasets** from Project STORM. Download the original Excel files from Zenodo. These are the source records, not files generated by HeritageGuard.

## 1. Hall I — original manuscript evaluation (E1–E4)

- **Official record:** https://zenodo.org/records/3256111
- **Versioned DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3256111
- **Workbook:** `Hall1_FBG_Dataset2017-2019.xlsx`
- **Published MD5:** `aa52a4f3d4421640a2b4e590f7bd4a02`
- **Site:** Hall I at the Baths of Diocletian, Rome
- **Recorded period:** October 2017 to May 2019, across 14 monthly sheets
- **Roles as documented by the source:** strain/lesion S0, S2, S3; masonry temperature S1, S8; masonry humidity S4–S7
- **Study role:** Original Hall I forecasting experiments, E1–E4

**Original dataset citation:** U. Santamaria, G. Governatori, S. Mastrostefano, E. Dell'Aglio and R. Reale, *Project STORM: monitoring the masonry of Hall I, at the Baths of Diocletian, with Fiber Bragg Grating sensors. RAW Dataset 2017–2019*, Zenodo, 2019. doi: [10.5281/zenodo.3256111](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3256111).

## 2. Michelangelo's Cloister — retrospective cross-location evaluation

- **Official record:** https://zenodo.org/records/3248893
- **Versioned DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3248893
- **Workbook:** `Cloister_FBG_Dataset2018-2019.xlsx`
- **Published MD5:** `2d54933990aba9b24f4b50444988b541`
- **Site:** Michelangelo's Cloister at the Baths of Diocletian, Rome
- **Recorded period:** October 2018 to May 2019, across 8 monthly sheets
- **Roles as documented by the source:** strain/lesion S0, S3; masonry temperature S1, S2, S8; masonry humidity S4–S7
- **Study role:** Retrospective transfer of Hall I models and retrospective within-Cloister model fitting

**Original dataset citation:** U. Santamaria, G. Governatori, S. Mastrostefano, E. Dell'Aglio and R. Reale, *Project STORM: monitoring the masonry of Michelangelo's Cloister, at the Baths of Diocletian, with Fiber Bragg Grating sensors. RAW Dataset 2018–2019*, Zenodo, 2019. doi: [10.5281/zenodo.3248893](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3248893).

## Download and verify

1. Open the relevant Zenodo record.
2. Download the named `.xlsx` workbook from its Files section.
3. Verify its MD5 checksum against the published value.

Example on macOS/Linux:

```bash
md5sum Hall1_FBG_Dataset2017-2019.xlsx
md5sum Cloister_FBG_Dataset2018-2019.xlsx
```

PowerShell on Windows:

```powershell
Get-FileHash .\Hall1_FBG_Dataset2017-2019.xlsx -Algorithm MD5
Get-FileHash .\Cloister_FBG_Dataset2018-2019.xlsx -Algorithm MD5
```

These two original workbooks were checked against the published MD5 digests before this dataset index was prepared.

## Data provenance and scientific boundaries

- This repository links to **both source workbooks**; it does not currently host raw copies or validated processed data.
- The original paper's E1–E4 evaluation results are retained as historical results. Their full original anomaly masks and exact split manifest are not available for complete reproduction.
- Reconstructed and cross-location results must be described as retrospective. Do not use their metrics to replace or restate original E1–E4 results.
- Both locations are within the Baths of Diocletian complex; they do not constitute independent-monument validation.
- The Zenodo records indicate **CC BY 4.0** for these datasets. Reuse requires attribution to the original authors.

## What is not a source dataset for this manuscript?

Other explored datasets, including experimental smart-brick measurements, are not part of the reported main Hall I / Cloister experiments and are deliberately not listed as manuscript input datasets here.
